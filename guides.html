Appliance Lifespan Expectancy

Average lifespan metrics and key guidelines to double their operational utility.

Appliance Avg Lifespan Maintenance Guideline
Ceiling & Exhaust Fans 10 Years Clean motor blades yearly; replace humming run capacitors immediately to prevent stator heat burnouts.
Steam & Dry Irons 5 Years Flush inner reservoirs using descaling solution every 6 months to prevent mineral scale deposits.
Kitchen Mixer Grinders 8 Years Never run continuously past 5 minutes; check overload cutoff button under chassis before restarting stalled blades.
Hot Water Geysers 12 Years Replace magnesium sacrificial anode rods every 3 years; execute total flush descaling cycles every 12 months.

Technician Advice FAQs

Answers to key care questions asked by our clients during diagnostics.

We strongly advise against DIY rewinding. Motor windings require calibrated wire tension and electrical varnish coating to avoid internal short circuits. A poorly wound stator poses severe fire hazards.
If you use normal tap water, we recommend descaling every 4 to 6 months. If you use distilled water, once a year is sufficient to prevent steam nozzle blockage.
Loud rumbling or knocking is a sign of severe sediment build-up. As water gets trapped beneath the mineral crust at the bottom, it boils and bursts out, making popping noises. A full flush and descaling is required.
