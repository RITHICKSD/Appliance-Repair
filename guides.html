Technician Advice FAQs

Answers to key care questions asked by our clients during diagnostics.

Is it safe to rewind a ceiling fan motor myself?

We strongly advise against DIY rewinding. Motor windings require calibrated wire tension and electrical varnish coating to avoid internal short circuits. A poorly wound stator poses severe fire hazards.

How often should I descale my steam iron?

If you use normal tap water, we recommend descaling every 4 to 6 months. If you use distilled water, once a year is sufficient to prevent steam nozzle blockage.

Why does my geyser make a loud rumbling sound?

Loud rumbling or knocking is a sign of severe sediment build-up. As water gets trapped beneath the mineral crust at the bottom, it boils and bursts out, making popping noises. A full flush and descaling is required.
