Common Geyser Diagnostics

We quickly analyze and repair the root causes of all water heater anomalies.

Water is Not Heating

Usually caused by a burnt heating element or a faulty safety cutoff thermostat. We carry original components to perform fast, on-site replacements.

Water Heater is Leaking

Leaking from the base indicates gasket decay or inner cylinder corrosion. We inspect gaskets, clean tank fittings, or recommend safe tank solutions.

Tripping the Circuit Breaker

A breaker trip signals a ground fault or short circuit inside the heating chamber. We use multimeters to check coil insulation before restarting.

Rumbling or Popping Sounds

Strange cracking sounds inside the tank are due to mineral scale buildup overheating local water pockets. Tank flushing and descaling removes this hazard.
